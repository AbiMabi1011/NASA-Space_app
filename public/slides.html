CHALLENGE & PROBLEM STATEMENT 02 / 07

The Problem: Agriculture in an Unpredictable Climate

Smallholder farmers in tropical and arid drylands face unprecedented seasonal rainfall volatility, prolonged dry spells, and depleted soils.

  • Monoculture Depletion: Continuous single-crop cultivation (like paddy rice) exhausts soil nitrogen and fuels pest infestations.
  • The Satellite Data Gap: NASA produces world-class agroclimatology, but it remains locked in complex gridded APIs out of reach for farmers.
  • Black-Box Distrust: Opaque AI yield predictions lack explainability and often hallucinate unviable crops.

SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE · STEP 1 03 / 07

Global Reach: Interactive Point-and-Click Map

FieldShift works anywhere on Earth with zero geographic boundaries.

  • Point-and-Click Map: Drop a pin anywhere on Earth or select curated agricultural presets (South Asia, Africa, Americas, Europe, Australia).
  • Soil & Field Profile: Captures soil texture (sandy, clay, silty loam), estimated pH (4.5–8.5), current main crop, and water access tier.
  • Live NASA POWER Query: Dropping a pin instantly queries NASA POWER’s global 0.5°×0.5° meteorological grid for that exact location.

NASA DATA & SCIENTIFIC RIGOR 04 / 07

10-Year Climatology & Satellite Cross-Check

We analyze 10 complete years of NASA Earth observations rather than relying on noisy short-term forecasts.

  • NASA POWER Agroclimatology API: 10-year monthly 2-meter air temperature (T2M) and precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) series.
  • Seasonal Drought Risk Ratio: Identifies seasons below 75% of the 10-year median rainfall to quantify dry-spell probability.
  • Independent GPM IMERG Cross-Check: Validates precipitation against NASA’s Global Precipitation Measurement satellite without blending sensors.

BIOPHYSICAL ENGINE · STEP 2 05 / 07

Explainable One-by-One Rotation Pathways

Transparent scoring ranks multi-season rotation pathways tailored to farmer goals.

  • Step-by-Step Stepper: Farmers review #1 Top Recommendation, #2 Strong Alternative, and #3 Diversified Option one by one.
  • Biological Rhizobia Nitrogen Fixation: Highlights pulses and green-manure crops (cowpea, sunn hemp) that replenish soil nitrogen and slash chemical fertilizer costs.
  • Dynamic Priority Re-Ranking: Instantly shifts recommendations when toggling between Save Water, Build Soil, and Steady Income.
