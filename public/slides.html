CHALLENGE & PROBLEM STATEMENT 02 / 07

The Problem: Agriculture in an Unpredictable Climate

Smallholder farmers in tropical and arid drylands face unprecedented seasonal rainfall volatility, prolonged dry spells, and depleted soils.

  • Monoculture Vulnerability: Continuous single-crop cultivation (like paddy rice) rapidly depletes soil nitrogen and increases pest/disease risk.
  • The Satellite Data Disconnect: While NASA collects world-class global agroclimatology, it remains locked in complex APIs and raw NetCDF/JSON arrays unreachable by farmers.
  • Black-Box Distrust: Opaque deep-learning models lack explainability and often hallucinate crop rotations unsuited to local water access and soil pH.
FieldShift bridges the gap between NASA satellites and local farmer decisions NASA Knights
SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE · STEP 1 03 / 07

Global Reach: Interactive Point-and-Click Map

FieldShift works anywhere on Earth with zero geographic boundaries.

  • Point-and-Click Leaflet Map: Farmers or extension officers can drop a pin anywhere on Earth or select curated agricultural presets (South Asia, Africa, Americas, Australia, Mediterranean).
  • Localized Field Notes: Captures soil texture (sandy loam, clay loam, silty loam), soil pH (4.5–8.5), current main crop, and water access tier.
  • Live NASA POWER Query: Dropping a pin instantly queries NASA POWER’s global 0.5°×0.5° meteorological grid for that exact location.

NASA DATA & SCIENTIFIC RIGOR 04 / 07

10-Year Climatology & Satellite Cross-Check

We analyze 10 complete years of NASA Earth observations rather than relying on noisy short-term weather forecasts.

  • NASA POWER Agroclimatology API: Retrieves 2-meter air temperature (T2M) and precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) across 120 historical months.
  • Seasonal Drought Risk Ratio: Identifies seasons falling below 75% of the 10-year median rainfall to quantify dry-spell probability.
  • Independent GPM IMERG Cross-Check: Validates precipitation against NASA’s Global Precipitation Measurement satellite without silently blending disparate sensors.
Transparent, verified satellite climate indicators with zero black-box tampering NASA Knights
BIOPHYSICAL ENGINE · STEP 2 05 / 07

Explainable One-by-One Rotation Pathways

Transparent scoring ranks multi-season rotation pathways tailored to farmer goals.

  • Step-by-Step Stepper: Farmers review #1 Top Recommendation, #2 Strong Alternative, and #3 Diversified Option one by one.
  • Biological Rhizobia Nitrogen Fixation: Highlights pulses and green-manure crops (cowpea, sunn hemp) that biologically replenish soil nitrogen and slash synthetic fertilizer costs.
  • Dynamic Priority Re-Ranking: Instantly shifts recommendations when toggling between Save Water, Build Soil, and Steady Income.
Grounded in agronomy: crop family alternation, thermal fit, and water conservation NASA Knights
MONITORING STUDIO & PORTABILITY 06 / 07

Field Monitoring & Printable Decision Card

FieldShift bridges digital telemetry with real-world field extension practices.

  • Satellite Field Simulation: 3D isometric zone dashboard tracking Zone A, B, and C moisture indices and triggering auto-irrigation alerts.
  • GPS-Linked Field Officer Console: Displays live coordinates, mean air temp, and agro-telemetry dynamically synchronized to the selected field.
  • One-Click Field Decision Card: Exports a printable 1-page HTML/PDF card with NASA climate signals, rotation sequences, and extension officer sign-off spaces.
Offline-friendly: printable cards empower farmers without reliable field internet NASA Knights
IMPACT & ROADMAP · CONCLUSION 07 / 07

Impact, Scalability & Roadmap

FieldShift transforms raw Earth observations into sustainable food security worldwide.

  • Global Scalability: Zero local hardware required; scales anywhere via NASA’s open-access planetary APIs.
  • Economic & Environmental ROI: Reduces chemical fertilizer expenditure via rhizobia nitrogen fixation while building drought resilience.
  • Future Roadmap:
    • Integration with NASA SMAP (Soil Moisture Active Passive) satellite radar.
    • Local vernacular audio readouts for non-literate farmers.
    • Progressive Web App (PWA) with SMS/USSD advisory integration.
